# Open Routines

**Automate the boring parts.**

Open Routines is a desktop app that lets you define repeatable AI workflows — routines — that run on your schedule and handle the busywork, so you don't have to.

---

## What it does

You describe what you want done. Open Routines runs it for you, automatically.

- A cron fires every weekday morning and drops a digest of your open PRs in your inbox.
- A folder watcher picks up a new invoice PDF the moment it lands and adds a row to your spreadsheet.
- A Gmail trigger scans your inbox for this year's invoices, pulls the PDFs, and builds you a colour-coded expense report.

The routine runs, the work gets done, you move on.

---

## Why Open Routines

**Your models, your compute.** Plug in your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key, or point it at a local model via Ollama. No subscription, no cloud dependency, no lock-in.

**Fine-grained control.** Each routine has its own permission set. Decide what it can read, write, execute, and fetch — independently. Set anything sensitive to *ask* and it will prompt you before acting. It won't delete your hard drive if it doesn't have shell access.

**Connected to the outside world.** Routines aren't limited to your file system. Connect Gmail and let them search your inbox, pull attachments, and act on what they find. More integrations on the way.
